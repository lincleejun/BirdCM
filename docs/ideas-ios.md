# BirdCM / 观羽 iOS: ranked feature candidates

Design research, 2026-11-18. **This is a decision menu, not implementation scope.** The required life list, correction, four tabs, offline species search, statistics, camera capture and Files backup belong to [design-lifelist.md](design-lifelist.md). None of the proposals below authorizes implementation or an online service. Ranking is a product judgement for a bird photographer, not a claim about competitors' popularity or measured user demand.

## What the products teach us

These are documented functions, not hands-on app tests. Product pages describe intended behavior; they do not establish accuracy, licensing permission to reuse content, or availability in every country. The useful principles are evidence, retrieval, preparation and reflection. Retain 观羽's native navigation, paper/pine palette, quiet photography and honest uncertainty from [ios/DESIGN.md](../ios/DESIGN.md); do not borrow another app's layouts, artwork, names, points or rarity vocabulary.

## What we can actually build from today's assets

The bundle already contains the global identification models, 11,172 AviList species names, genus/family fields in `names.tsv`, BirdNET geographical priors, and regional/month GBIF tiers plus optional IUCN categories. There is no bundled species-photo, call-recording or descriptive-text library. Some Chinese names are absent (the current NOTICE records 59); show the English/Latin fallback. There is no order field in the currently consumed catalogue. The existing iOS app is approximately 698 MiB installed; "regional packs" will not make its global visual tower smaller without a new model strategy. See [README](../ios/README.md), [validation](../ios/VALIDATION.md), [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md) and [Engine.swift](../ios/BirdCM/Engine.swift).

A free Apple Personal Team constrains extension designs that depend on App Groups as well as iCloud services. Do not make widgets or share extensions a hidden requirement of the baseline. Verify each target's actual provisioning on the owner's team before committing to it; choose main-app App Intents and Files handoff where feasible. [Apple capability table](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/reference/supported-capabilities-ios/), [App Intents](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents), [WidgetKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit), [ActivityKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit).

## Ranked proposals

S = a small local feature using existing records; M = a new workflow or schema addition; L = a new model, service, device target or substantial import pipeline. These are relative engineering estimates, including accessibility and verification, not calendar promises. Risk abbreviations: **L** licence/content rights; **A** identification or statistical accuracy; **P** privacy. Unless explicitly described as online, each candidate should work locally. Every new datum must enter the one canonical store and portable export; no parallel history.

## The next three I would choose

1. **Outings** create the missing unit of experience: the owner remembers a trip, not a database row. They improve retrieval and provide a sound foundation for later effort, targets and reports without adding a model or service.
2. **Look-alike comparison** directly addresses the known Royal/Elegant Tern failure. It turns correction into learning, uses the existing candidate distributions and original crops, and can ship initially with no external descriptive content.
3. **Year-in-review sharing** makes the new statistics tangible with a small amount of local rendering work. A deliberate preview lets the owner choose the photographs and remove place details before sharing.

Build these only after baseline migration, correction, counting and backup are accepted. Ranks 4–12 mostly deepen the existing private photo workflow. The lower-ranked ideas are useful, but introduce services, licensing, provisioning or device targets before we have evidence that the owner needs them.

## Gates for the larger proposals

**Sound ID.** BirdNET-Analyzer documents MIT for its source and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for its models, including an instruction to check the exact model's terms. That does not grant a commercial licence or resolve how an app should satisfy ShareAlike for a converted model. Select a specific acoustic release, archive its licence/labels/checksum, check conversion and redistribution obligations, and resolve intended distribution before bundling it. The existing geomodel's conflicting licence statements in [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md) are a separate unresolved issue, not evidence that acoustic weights are Apache-2.0. [BirdNET licensing FAQ](https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer/blob/main/docs/faq.rst). Prototype accuracy and battery on the owner's real iPhone with recordings whose rights are documented; do not claim parity with the BirdNET app merely by adopting its model family.

**Nearby/recent eBird data.** The official API terms require an account-bound key, forbid sharing that key with third parties, require eBird attribution and restrict commercial use without prior written permission. The linked Data Access terms also restrict redistribution of original datasets and govern derived datasets. A future implementation must not commit a key, ship someone else's key or package API responses into public regional packs. Start with a browser link, then scope any optional adapter against the exact access and caching terms; avoid bulk polling, label fetched-at time and preserve sensitive-species restrictions. This is online discovery, not offline evidence or synchronization. [API terms](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/ebird-api-terms-of-use/), [Data Access terms](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/ebird-data-access-terms-of-use/).

**Regional content.** No competitor's image gallery, field marks, distribution artwork or sound recordings may be scraped. A browser link is not a licence to cache the destination. Packs require owner-authored or explicitly licensed content and a per-asset manifest; preserve current GBIF, AviList, Chinese-name, IUCN and model notices. Do not add a third-party dependency solely to render a small chart, card or comparison.

**Privacy.** None of these candidates implies an account, public feed or upload. Social images should omit sensitive coordinates; archival backups must preserve the owner's raw facts and clearly disclose that selected file providers may store them remotely. A future public contribution flow requires its own preview and explicit destination choice.

## Decisions for the owner, when choosing the next feature

- Is an outing mainly a named photo album, or do you want to record actual birding effort and birds without photographs?
- For comparison, would you supply trusted reference photos/field notes, or should the first version use only your existing crops and personal notes?
- Which matters first after the baseline: learning look-alikes, importing a day's camera burst, or planning places to visit?
- Would sound ID remain a personal/noncommercial tool, and are you willing to resolve the chosen model's redistribution terms before sharing builds?
- Should any future online discovery use a personal eBird key, or should 观羽 stay entirely offline apart from browser/map requests?

Research limit: documentation and publisher listings were checked on 2026-11-18; no competitor account was used, no paid content was accessed, no accuracy benchmark was run, and no new capability was tested on Personal Team 3U353L39GB. These limits do not block the separate offline life-list specification.
